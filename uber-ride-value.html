Project Detail

UberRideValue

Analyzing Uber ride booking data to understand value drivers, pricing patterns, and cancellation behavior.

View GitHub Repo Back to Projects

Summary

What the project was trying to answer.

This project analyzes Uber ride booking data to predict booking value and uncover the factors that influence pricing. It also looks at what drives cancellations and incomplete rides so the business impact can be understood beyond a simple fare estimate.

The work combines exploratory analysis, modeling, and visual storytelling. The main takeaway is that ride distance and wait time are strongly connected to both price and cancellation outcomes, which creates a tradeoff between maximizing value and maintaining a reliable rider experience.

Key findings

  • Ride distance and trip time are strong pricing drivers.
  • Long pickup waits are strongly associated with cancellations.
  • Customer ratings declined over time, signaling reliability issues.
  • Decision tree models were chosen because the relationships were not linear.

Analytical takeaway

What the analysis suggests.

Pricing and value

Ride distance, travel time, and booking context are the strongest contributors to booking value. That makes pricing highly dependent on trip characteristics rather than a single simple factor.

Cancellation risk

A key pattern is that long pickup waits drive more incomplete rides and no-driver-found outcomes. This suggests a practical operational threshold for better matching and ride assignment.

Customer experience

The long-run rating trend indicates that service reliability matters just as much as revenue and trip efficiency. Better cancellation management would likely improve both satisfaction and retention.
